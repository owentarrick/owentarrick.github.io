A nice thing happened at work

19 May 2026

Not a cycling post, sorry.

We had our half-year review session this week and Serena, my manager, gave me a proper shout-out in front of the whole Treasury team for the settlement reconciliation work. I have been reporting to her since I joined and she is not one for handing out praise casually, so it landed.

Four years in and I still think this was a good decision. Small firm, you get trusted with real work early, and nobody is precious about job titles.
