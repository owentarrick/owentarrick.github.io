New PB on the Braamfontein Spruit

12 July 2026

Out at half six this morning to beat the dog walkers and finally cracked the Delta Park loop in under forty minutes. Two degrees on the Wahoo at the start, frost still sitting on the grass in the open sections, and absolutely worth it.

Straight off the phone, no filter. That first light on the highveld in July is unbeatable.

Home, shower, and at the desk by half seven to get the overnight settlement file reconciled before anyone else is in. There is something quite satisfying about having the whole liquidity position squared away before the first coffee round.

Biscuit update: six months in

28 June 2026

Six months since we brought Biscuit home from the rescue and I can report that the greyhound stereotype is entirely accurate. Eighteen hours of sleep a day, then approximately forty seconds of absolutely terrifying speed across the garden.

He has learned that the sound of the turbo trainer means I am not going anywhere for an hour, so he has claimed the couch for the duration. He has also learned that load-shedding means the trainer stops, which he treats as a personal victory.

My other half keeps pointing out that Biscuit is now the answer to about six of my security questions. She is not wrong and I should probably do something about that.

The Rosebank commute, ranked

3 June 2026

We went to a split-site arrangement last year, so I am at the Rosebank office on Tuesdays and Thursdays and in Sandton the rest of the week. Two very different rides.

Rosebank days I go through the suburbs, which is longer but far more pleasant. Sandton days I take the main road because it is quicker and I am usually running late.

The Tuesday ride is the best one of the week. Nobody schedules anything before ten on a Tuesday, so I can take the slow route and still be at my desk before the phone starts.
