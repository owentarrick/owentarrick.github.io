New PB on the Braamfontein Spruit

12 July 2026

Out at half six this morning to beat the dog walkers and finally cracked the Delta Park loop in under forty minutes. Two degrees on the Wahoo at the start, frost still sitting on the grass in the open sections, and absolutely worth it.

Straight off the phone, no filter. That first light on the highveld in July is unbeatable.

Home, shower, and at the desk by half seven to get the overnight settlement file reconciled before anyone else is in. There is something quite satisfying about having the whole liquidity position squared away before the first coffee round.
